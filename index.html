About Me

Engineering AI that is grounded, measurable and useful.

I'm an Artificial Intelligence and Robotics graduate (2026) who builds end-to-end machine learning systems — from data cleaning and model training to deployed interfaces. Across eight hands-on projects I have shipped retrieval-augmented chatbots, a multi-agent customer-support platform, BERT-based text classifiers and medical image models, working daily with Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain and FastAPI.

My philosophy is simple: a model only matters when it solves a real problem reliably. That means grounding LLM answers in verified knowledge, handling imbalanced and messy data honestly, evaluating with the right metrics, and wrapping every model in an interface people can actually use. That same mindset drives my web work: I build fast, accessible, SEO-ready websites — such as the Arabic site for Noon Student Services — with automated tests and a perfect Lighthouse score. I'm especially driven by Arabic-language AI, where thoughtful engineering can open technology to millions of underserved users.
